## BeamNG.Earth research for any future implementation

The removal TODO above supersedes the traffic and weather proposals. These notes document constraints for a future design.

- Traffic. BeamNG.Earth's navgraph manages any running traffic itself: it moves cars that stand still 45 s (our traffic is held still through prep), hides all traffic after a teleport until the new road graph arrives (round setup moves cops far), the road graph arrives after the round car is placed (a moved cop's traffic finds no roads), and a region change sets traffic up again with the player's own amount and police choice. Traffic positions would also have to travel as latitude/longitude for the crash fault rule
- Night and weather rounds: go through Earth's server hooks (`beamngEarth_sky.setServerClock`, `beamngEarth_weather.setPreset`), not `core_environment`, which flips players to Manual and saves it in their Earth settings. Earth ignores the date, has six weather presets that take about 60 s to fade in, and leaves players on Manual alone. Research notes in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-night-weather-design.md`
